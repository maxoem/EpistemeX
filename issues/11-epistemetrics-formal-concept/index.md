# 11 — Epistemetrics Formal & Conceptual Metric Audit

Systematic formal, mathematical, conceptual, and metascientific evaluation of the metric specifications in [`docs/concepts/theory/metrics/`](../../docs/concepts/theory/metrics/).

Each metric specification is evaluated across four foundational dimensions:
1. **Construct Validity:** Does the metric capture what it claims it does?
2. **Formal & Mathematical Correctness:** Are all equations well-typed, bounded as claimed, invariant under index permutations, and free of unbound variables or division-by-zero traps?
3. **Semantic Stability:** Does the specification avoid silent semantic shifts between incompatible ontological levels (e.g. structuralist set-theoretic models vs. statement-view propositions vs. property-graph node counts)?
4. **Scientific Significance & Architectural Fit:** Is the metric scientifically grounded in the philosophy of science literature, non-redundant with other Episteme metrics, and operationally computable on `TheoryGraph`?

**Severity Legend:**
- **Critical** = Invalidates the metric's mathematical/conceptual core, yields inverted diagnostics, or is mathematically uncomputable as written.
- **Major** = Significant formal flaw, semantic shift, gaming vulnerability, or literature misattribution that must be resolved before implementation/publication.
- **Minor** = Notation collision, missing edge-case guard, LaTeX corruption, or schema label misalignment.
