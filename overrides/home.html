Research Horizons

We are constantly evolving — take a look at our roadmap and research horizons. The following are our current short-term targets.

01

Evaluation Harness

Standardizing automated benchmarks with soft semantic matching (G-BS, OEP) and human baseline calibration to ensure scientific rigor.

02

Jev Classification & Gating

Offloading pipeline decisions to non-generative, typed inference. Uses calibrated confidence scores to gate gleaning and reasoning with minimal latency.

Frontier Dynamics

Exploring neuro-symbolic theory synthesis, contrastive candidate retrieval, and 4D hypercomplex relational embeddings.
